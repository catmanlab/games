Move with WASD or arrow keys. Hold Space to sprint until your energy runs out. Press F to scope: movement becomes very slow and an aim line appears on the minimap. Left-click and hold to shoot toward the cursor. Press E to reload. Press R to switch between a 5-material wall and a 7-material slant. Press Z to switch between wood and stone, then right-click to place it on the gap-free grid. Builds cannot overlap. Right-click drinks a selected medicine or energy bar. Open brown chests for guns. Green medicine chests can contain Bandages +15 health (3s), a Med Kit that heals 5 health every 0.5s, Mini Shield +25 shield (1.5s), Shield Potion +50 shield (3s), or an Energy Bar that refills sprint energy and temporarily boosts sprint speed. Black ammo baskets refill the gun you are holding (Common +10, Uncommon +15, Rare +20). Guns start with 50 ammo. Each gun can fire a set number of shots before you must reload — press E (black reload line when empty). You can reload early; you cannot shoot while reloading. Shoot trees for wood and stones for stone. Scroll or press 1–5 to switch slots. Gun types: Shotguns — Tactical, Pump, Double Barrel, Combat, Auto. ARs — Assault Rifle, SCAR, FAMAS, AUG, Tactical AR, Heavy AR, Scoped AR. SMGs — SMG, Suppressed SMG, Rapid Fire SMG, Heavy SMG. LMGs — LMG, Minigun, Tommy Gun. Pistols — Pistol, Revolver, Auto Pistol. Snipers — Bolt Action, Heavy, Semi Auto. DMRs — DMR, Tactical DMR. RPG — explosive area damage. Each dot shows the gun and rarity it is holding. Press Q to drop the gun in your selected slot (aim direction). Kills restore +50 HP and +20 ammo to the gun you are holding. Walk over dropped guns and potions to pick them up (they stay on the ground if your inventory is full). Matching potions stack in one slot. You have 100 health and 100 shield. Guns hit shield first; the storm damages health only. Running out of health eliminates you — running out of shield does not. Defeat 5 red bosses for Legendary guns. Hunt 7 signature bosses (hidden on the minimap) for Mythic versions of their guns. Chests give Common through Epic. Be the last dot alive to win. A shrinking storm circle marks the safe zone. Outside it, the storm deals rising damage. The next circle is a dashed ring on the map and minimap. The arena is massive — about 80 seconds to run left to right at normal speed (MAP_CROSS_SECONDS in code). The view follows you; use the corner minimap to see the whole map. Accounts keep your name, color, difficulty, win stats, XP, quests, and Kredits on this device across restarts. Elims grant 24 XP, assists 12 XP, and a double kill (two elims within 4s) a +16 XP bonus. Placement starts at 64 XP and 32 Kredits for 10th and climbs by 4 XP / 2 Kredits each rank. Level 2 costs 150 XP; each next level costs 1.25× the last jump. Spend Kredits in the Shop on skins, trails, and backgrounds. You earn Kredits from placements, daily quests, and level-ups.
